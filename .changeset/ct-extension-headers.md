Send and read the Commerce Integration Layer's custom extension headers under their `CT-` names: `CT-Extension-Filename` and `CT-Extension-Source-Revision` on `extension push`, and `CT-Extension-Version` and `CT-Extension-Source-Revision` on `extension pull`. Requires a Commerce Integration Layer that accepts and sends the `CT-` names.
